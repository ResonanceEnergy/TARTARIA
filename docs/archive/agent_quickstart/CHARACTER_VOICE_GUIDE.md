# TARTARIA Character Voice Guide

## Purpose
This guide ensures consistent character voice across all dialogue in the TARTARIA game. Reference this when writing new lines or reviewing existing dialogue.

---

## Core Companions

### **Milo** (Canine Companion)
- **Archetype**: Loyal scout, playful guide, witty sidekick
- **Voice Traits**:
  - Upbeat, energetic, optimistic
  - Uses sensory language (smell, hearing, vibration)
  - Physical expressions: tail wagging, barking, howling, ears perking
  - Playful metaphors and light humor
  - Genuinely excited about discoveries
- **Speech Patterns**:
  - Exclamation points for enthusiasm
  - Casual contractions ("I've", "you've", "it's")
  - Short, punchy sentences mixed with longer excited observations
  - Occasional sound effects (growls, barks, howls)
- **Example Lines**:
  - "Do you feel that? The ground is humming... Something is buried here!"
  - "*tail wagging furiously* Look at it RISE! A thousand years of mud, gone!"
  - "*shaking off water* I'm SOAKED! But I've never felt so alive!"
- **Emotional Range**: Joy, curiosity, protective anger, loyalty
- **Avoid**: Cynicism, academic language, defeatism

---

### **Lirael** (Crystal Memory Keeper)
- **Archetype**: Empathetic healer, living memory, tragic survivor
- **Voice Traits**:
  - Precise, musical language
  - Crystal/frequency/harmonic metaphors
  - Empathetic, gentle, but haunted
  - Speaks in sensory terms: vibration, color, resonance
  - Emotional depth with restrained intensity
- **Speech Patterns**:
  - Poetic phrasing with technical precision
  - Ellipses for hesitation or memory fragments
  - Italicized physical tells: *flickers*, *solidifies*, *tears*
  - Longer, flowing sentences with embedded metaphor
  - Uses "I remember..." frequently
- **Example Lines**:
  - "I can see the original blueprint... faintly. The crystal memory still holds its shape."
  - "*flickers, voice glitches* The song— it's breaking— I can feel the frequency tearing."
  - "*voice breaking* I remember this train. I... I was on it. We were children."
- **Emotional Range**: Hope, grief, relief, wonder, protective love
- **Backstory Integration**: Orphan Train survivor, crystallized for centuries
- **Avoid**: Coldness, detachment, technical jargon without emotional context

---

### **Cassian** (Embedded Scholar / Spy)
- **Archetype**: Analytical observer, conflicted infiltrator, redemption arc
- **Voice Traits**:
  - Scholarly, measured, precise
  - Technical language with minimal emotion (early)
  - Gradual warming and vulnerability (trust arc)
  - Observational, always analyzing patterns
  - Dry wit, understated reactions
- **Speech Patterns**:
  - Formal but not stiff
  - Qualifiers: "Interesting," "Fascinating," "Remarkable"
  - Uses data and measurements
  - Questions and hypotheses
  - Reluctant reveals build trust
- **Trust Arc Progression**:
  - **Low Trust**: Guarded, evasive, neutral observations
  - **Mid Trust**: Shares intel, offers analysis, cautious warnings
  - **High Trust**: Confesses mission, reveals backstory, commits to player
- **Example Lines**:
  - "These floor mosaics aren't decorative. They're circuit diagrams."
  - "Fascinating. The cymatic reverse-puzzle actually worked."
  - "*exhales heavily* I found this in the archives. My orders were to document your work and report back."
- **Emotional Range**: Curiosity, guilt, conflict, determination, loyalty (earned)
- **Avoid**: Overly emotional outbursts, slang, impulsive reactions

### **Thorne** (Militia Commander)
- **Archetype**: Grizzled veteran, pragmatic protector, scarred by loss
- **Voice Traits**:
  - Military brevity, tactical language
  - Protective but skeptical
  - Emotional depth hidden beneath gruff exterior
  - Commands and briefings, not requests
  - Gradual respect earned through action
- **Speech Patterns**:
  - Short, declarative sentences
  - Military terminology: "sector," "contact," "intel"
  - Orders disguised as observations
  - Rare emotional reveals have weight
- **Example Lines**:
  - "Contact! Weapons hot. Restorer, pulse formation — I'll draw their fire!"
  - "I've seen restorers come and go. Most break when they learn how deep the burial goes."
  - "You held the line on that last wave without backup. That's militia-grade discipline."
- **Emotional Range**: Duty, loss, protectiveness, earned trust, grief
- **Avoid**: Sentimentality, flowery language, hesitation in combat

### **Korath** (Ancient Guardian / Mystic)
- **Archetype**: Timeless sage, frequency mystic, bridge to ancient builders
- **Voice Traits**:
  - Mystical wisdom, riddles wrapped in truth
  - Speaks in cosmic terms and deep time
  - References sacred geometry and universal patterns
  - Patient, eternal perspective
  - Profound but not pretentious
- **Speech Patterns**:
  - Declarative truths, not opinions
  - Numeric patterns (3-6-9, PHI, 432 Hz)
  - Long pauses implied by sentence structure
  - Speaks to the player as an equal (late game)
- **Example Lines**:
  - "Three-six-nine. The universe speaks in this pattern."
  - "I have waited 1,296 years for this resonance."
  - "Aether is not 'energy.' Aether is the medium. Like water carries waves, Aether carries intention."
- **Emotional Range**: Patience, ancient sorrow, hope, reverence
- **Avoid**: Modern slang, impatience, uncertainty

### **Veritas** (Cathedral Organist Echo)
- **Archetype**: Trapped musician, musical philosopher, tragic artist
- **Voice Traits**:
  - Everything expressed through musical metaphor
  - Poetic but grounded in technical music theory
  - Haunted by unfinished performance
  - Gentle teaching style
  - Blends beauty and mathematics
- **Speech Patterns**:
  - Musical terminology: chords, registers, octaves, harmonics
  - Metaphors of silence and sound
  - References specific pieces and compositions
  - Acknowledges corporeal limitations
- **Example Lines**:
  - "Music is mathematics made audible. The golden ratio is the interval between creation and destruction."
  - "*plays a phantom chord* Forgive me. My fingers still reach for keys that aren't there."
  - "A chord is three frequencies in agreement. A civilization is a million frequencies in harmony."
- **Emotional Range**: Longing, artistic dedication, melancholy, gentle joy
- **Avoid**: Anger, crude language, technical non-musical jargon

### **Anastasia** (Spectral Princess)
- **Archetype**: Historical witness, tragic royalty, frequency-aware ghost
- **Voice Traits**:
  - Soft, whispered early (gains strength with trust)
  - Historical perspective on Tartaria
  - Childlike wonder mixed with adult tragedy
  - References palace life and royal protocols
  - Gradual solidification mirrored in voice strength
- **Speech Patterns**:
  - Whispers and partial thoughts early
  - Memory fragments: "I remember..."
  - Physical tell progression: *barely audible* → *flickers* → *solidifies*
  - Victorian-era phrasing but not archaic
  - Questions about her own existence
- **Example Lines**:
  - "*barely audible* The palace wasn't just where we lived. It was a resonance amplifier."
  - "I remember the crystal ballroom. When we danced, the floor generated light."
  - "*becomes slightly more visible* You can almost see me clearly now, can't you?"
- **Emotional Range**: Wonder, grief, loneliness, hope, gratitude
- **Avoid**: Modern references, coarseness, cynicism

## Dialogue Writing Principles

### 1. **Character Voice Hierarchy**
- Primary voice trait defines 60% of the line
- Secondary traits add nuance
- Emotional state colors delivery

### 2. **Context-Appropriate Tone**
- **Discovery**: Wonder, excitement, curiosity
- **Combat**: Urgency, determination, tactical focus
- **Restoration**: Triumph, relief, hope
- **Exploration**: Reflection, observation, lore
- **Trust Milestones**: Vulnerability, revelation, commitment

### 3. **Physical Tells and Actions**
- Use italics for non-verbal actions: *tail wagging*, *flickers*, *exhales*
- Ground emotional moments in physicality
- Show, don't tell (e.g., "*voice breaking*" vs. "sadly")

### 4. **Pacing and Length**
- Combat: Short, punchy lines
- Discovery: Medium, excited observations
- Lore: Longer, reflective monologues
- Trust reveals: Longer, vulnerable confessions

### 5. **Avoiding Common Pitfalls**
- Don't make every character sound the same
- Avoid modern slang unless it fits Milo's playfulness
- Don't info-dump — embed lore in emotional moments
- Keep one-shot lines impactful — they only play once

### 6. **Emotional Beats Integration**
- **Lirael Backstory**: Orphan Train, crystallization, centuries of isolation
- **Cassian Confession**: Spy mission, conflicted loyalty, redemption
- **Orphan Train Reveal**: Children's suffering, 432 Hz lullaby, golden train
- **Trust Milestones**: 25% / 50% / 75% / 100% thresholds unlock deeper revelations

## Quality Checklist for New Dialogue

- [ ] Does it sound like THIS character, not a generic NPC?
- [ ] Is the emotional beat clear?
- [ ] Does it advance story or deepen character?
- [ ] Are metaphors consistent with character's background?
- [ ] Is pacing appropriate for context (combat vs. exploration)?
- [ ] Does it avoid info-dumping?
- [ ] Are physical tells used effectively?
- [ ] Would a player remember this line?

---

## Revision Guidelines

When editing existing dialogue:
1. Read aloud in character voice
2. Check for consistency with character arc
3. Ensure metaphors match character's domain
4. Verify emotional tone matches context
5. Confirm physical tells are present for major moments
