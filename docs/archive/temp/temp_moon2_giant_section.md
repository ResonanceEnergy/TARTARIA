### Giant Mode Power Fantasies — Macro Scale in the Crystal Cathedral (Moon 2 Exclusive)

While **Micro-Giant Mode** allows the player to shrink and navigate the impossible fractal interiors of domes to purge corruption at its roots, **Giant Mode** (the 15–25 ft bloodline growth) delivers the ultimate external power fantasy: the player becomes a Titan walking among the living crystal structures of the corrupted cathedral.

These synergies and moments are **thematically unique to Moon 2's crystal/corruption environment** (fractal veins, dissonance crystals, 5-building ley grid, breathing dome). They turn the cathedral itself into a giant-scale playground and battlefield. Giant Mode in Moon 2 feels majestic, destructive in a restorative way, and deeply tied to the visual language of "golden light burning corruption like fire along a fuse."

All moments leverage the R7 living crystal visuals (per-building fractal veins, dome breathing, caustics, godrays) and interact with the CorruptionSystem and VFXController for spectacular, permanent world payoffs.

#### The 5–6 Signature Moon 2 Giant Mode Moments & Synergies

**1. Resonance Crystal Shatter Stomp**  
In giant form, double-tap / dedicated stomp input near exposed dissonance crystal clusters or the base of any moon2_* structure.  
- The ground cracks. Massive crystal formations explode outward in slow-motion shard cascades.  
- Each shard carries resonance energy, striking and igniting nearby corruption veins in beautiful fuse-burn chains.  
- Multiple nearby buildings receive instant partial purges.  
- Visual: flying crystal debris catches the light, caustics flare, low-frequency impact rumble.  
- Thematic fit: The giant literally shatters the corruption that the micro-giant player has to carefully dismantle from inside. Perfect duality.

**2. Corruption Vein Manipulation (Giant Hand Yank)**  
While giant, the player can target and "grab" glowing fractal corruption veins visible on the cathedral walls, floor, or fountain basin (via hold input).  
- Giant hands physically rip the vein clusters free like diseased roots.  
- Immediate chain reaction: veins across the targeted structure and adjacent ones ignite in the signature "burn like fire along a fuse" effect (R7 thickness-aware variants fire simultaneously).  
- Strong haptic "tearing" feedback + triumphant audio sting.  
- Synergy: Combines with Architect tree (faster subsequent restoration) and leaves permanent visual state (less black veins, more golden crystalline highlights).

**3. The Cathedral Quake — Major "Cathedral-Shaking" Sequence (Signature Power Fantasy)**  
The centerpiece moment of Moon 2 Giant Mode.  
Trigger: While in Giant Mode, perform a charged Resonance Stomp + Harmonic Roar directly against the base or flank of the Fractured Cathedral Dome (moon2_cathedral_dome).  
- **Phase 1 — The Impact**: The earth buckles. The entire dome visibly lurches. Violent vertical breathing pulse (amplified R7 dome breathing). Camera pulls back dramatically. Deep sub-bass quake through haptics and audio that players will feel in their chests.  
- **Phase 2 — Harmonic Cascade**: All five structures (dome, bell tower, fountain, crystal hall, ley chamber) begin synchronized resonance shakes. Golden light pulses race along every ley line and vein between them. Corruption veins across the entire zone simultaneously catch fire and burn away in one glorious wave.  
- **Phase 3 — The Great Breath & Shatter Finale**: The dome performs an exaggerated, slow, majestic breathing cycle. Crystal growth bursts from the dome crown (R7 crystal growth VFX enhanced). Massive purified Aether shockwave radiates outward, partially restoring nearby buildings and stunning all remaining fractal wraiths.  
- Rewards: Large RS burst (32+), permanent visual upgrade to the cathedral (stronger golden caustics, slower/more serene breathing on future restores), lore echo (pre-Flood giant memory inscription revealed on dome interior), and a unique "Titan of the Crystal Cathedral" title/ cosmetic.  
- Feel: "I didn't just fight the corruption — I shook the world back into harmony." This is the moment players remember as the peak of Moon 2.

**4. Massive Scale Exploration — Fractal Facet & Upper Ley Revelation**  
At full giant height, previously invisible massive crystal facets on the upper dome, bell tower spire, and ley chamber roof become interactable.  
- Tap / reach to "reveal" hidden pre-Flood giant-scale inscriptions and ley convergence diagrams etched into the crystal.  
- Grants temporary buffs (increased resonance radius, corruption resistance) and deep lore.  
- Only possible in Giant Mode — human scale players see only blank stone. Reinforces the "the giants built for giants" theme and gives exploration depth to the vertical cathedral space.

**5. Ley Resonance Bridge Stomp**  
Giant footsteps deliberately placed along the surface ley lines connecting the five buildings cause temporary glowing crystal bridge manifestations to appear between structures.  
- Bridges auto-purge minor corruption underfoot and provide safe elevated traversal paths.  
- Visual: sparkling ley crystal extrusions rise and connect during the stomp, then slowly fade — pure spectacle.  
- Synergy with Micro-Giant: A giant player can create a bridge that later helps companions or allows faster repositioning for the next micro-giant interior run.

**6. Micro-Macro Scale Synergy Roar (Optional Bridge Ability)**  
While giant outside a building, the Harmonic Roar ability can force-purge a single deep interior corruption node without requiring the player to enter Micro-Giant Mode.  
- Creates a beautiful visual of the roar propagating inward through the fractal lattice (godrays flare inside).  
- Rewards players who invest in both Guardian tree and Moon 2 exploration. Perfect thematic pairing of the two "giant" mechanics unique to this moon.

#### Integration & Progression Notes
- These abilities are unlocked/enhanced via the GUARDIAN skill tree (Stomp Damage, Tectonic Slam, Continental Stomp nodes) but receive Moon 2–specific visual and mechanical upgrades only while the player is inside the Crystalline Caverns.
- Giant Mode in Moon 2 directly feeds the "cathedral-shaking" climax of the Moon-End Spectacle (The Fractal Corruption Purge) — a successful Cathedral Quake can even shorten or dramatically alter the final phase of the Moon 2 boss fight.
- Ties to visuals: Every Giant action in Moon 2 triggers or amplifies the R7 living effects (dome breathing intensity, fuse variants, crystal growth, recursive lighting).
- Haptics & Audio: All moments use intense, low-frequency, crystalline impact sounds and prolonged rumbles to sell the scale.
- Power Fantasy Goal: In Moon 2 the player goes from "I am small and must sneak inside the corruption" (micro) to "I am colossal and the corruption fears me" (macro Giant) — the most satisfying swing in the entire campaign.

These moments make Giant Mode in the crystal/corruption cathedral feel uniquely powerful, restorative, and unforgettable.
