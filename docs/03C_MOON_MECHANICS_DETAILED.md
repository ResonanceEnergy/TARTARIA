## Moon Mechanics Overview

Each Moon follows a **28-day in-game cycle** with four 7-day phases (weeks):
- **Week 1 (Days 1–7):** Introduction — new mechanic tutorial, exploration, first encounters
- **Week 2 (Days 8–14):** Escalation — mechanic depth increases, companion development, mystery deepens
- **Week 3 (Days 15–21):** Crisis — combat intensifies, moral choices, major revelations
- **Week 4 (Days 22–28):** Crescendo — Moon-ending spectacle, boss encounter, cosmic event

**Universal Systems Active Every Moon:**
- Aether Harvesting (9-band spectrum, 432 Hz base tuning)
- Resonance Grid Progress (percentage tracked globally)
- 17-Hour Clock (alignment windows for bonus events)
- Companion Banter (context-sensitive, mood-reactive)
- Dissonance Threat Level (escalates Moon over Moon)

## Moon 1 — Magnetic Moon: Awakening

*Theme: Discovery, First Awe, Bloodline Activation*

### Core Restoration Mechanics

**Resonance Scanning**
- Tap-and-hold on buried structures to emit a sonar-like resonance pulse
- Pulse reveals structure outlines under mud layers — glow intensity indicates Aether concentration
- Deeper structures require longer holds (1s surface → 3s deep vault)
- HUD overlay: wireframe ghost of buried building appears, color-coded by restoration difficulty
  - Green: Minor debris clearing (5 minutes)
  - Yellow: Structural reconstruction needed (15 minutes)
  - Red: Deep Aether corruption — requires purification first (30+ minutes)

**Mud Excavation**
- Progressive dig mechanic: swipe gestures clear mud in layers
- Haptic feedback intensifies as you approach intact architecture
- Discovery moments: first glimpse of ornate stonework triggers slow-mo + choir sting
- Environmental storytelling: mud layers contain artifacts (newspaper fragments, broken tools, children's toys) that piece together the Flood narrative

**Dome Tuning**
- Restored domes require harmonic calibration via touch-wheel interface
- Match the dome's natural resonance frequency (mini-game: waveform alignment)
- Successfully tuned domes emit visible Aether columns — these connect to the global ley-line grid
- First dome tuning triggers a cinematic: golden light erupts upward, nearby bells ring sympathetically, fountains spontaneously activate

**Giant-Mode Burst**
- Triggered by giant skeleton "key" discovery (DLC 1 tie-in)
- 60-second transformation: player grows to giant scale, can move megaliths bare-handed
- Giant perspective reveals hidden inscriptions on building tops, invisible from ground level
- Cooldown: 1 full in-game day between uses

### Companion Layer

**Milo (Fox Spirit)**
- Introduced Day 1 — emerges from the first dome's ventilation shaft
- **Ability:** Highlights buried Aether veins (passive aura, 15m radius)
- **Banter triggers:** First excavation, first dome tuning, first giant-mode, nightfall
- **Personality:** Snarky, protective, deeply loyal — covers fear with humor
- **Growth:** Week 1 cautious → Week 4 actively leads player to discoveries

**Lirael (Spectral Architect)**
- Introduced Day 7 — manifests during first dome tuning
- **Ability:** Projects holographic blueprints of original structures over ruins
- **Banter triggers:** Architecture discovery, lore fragment, emotional story beat

### Moon-End Spectacle (Day 28)

**The Dome Awakening**
- First fully restored cathedral dome performs its inaugural resonance broadcast
- Sequence: cathedral fills with golden light → dome crown ignites with Aether → beam shoots skyward → nearby buried structures partially surface, drawn upward by sympathetic resonance → aurora bands appear overhead

## Moon 2 — Lunar Moon: Shadow & Purge (ENHANCED — Moon 2 Bosses & Major Encounters R7)

*Theme: Corruption, Internal Threat, First Real Loss — now with production-quality climactic cavern encounters*

### Core Restoration Mechanics

**Dissonance Detection**
- New tool: Dissonance Lens — toggleable overlay that reveals corruption veins in structures
- Corruption appears as dark fractal patterns spreading through stone and metal
- Detection range increases with Aether investment (upgradeable 10m → 25m → 50m)
- Corrupted zones cause haptic "wrongness" — irregular vibrations that feel off-rhythm

**Micro-Giant Mode**
- Inverse of giant mode: shrink to enter fractal interior architecture of domes
- Inside, domes are impossibly vast — cathedral-within-cathedral geometry
- Navigate crystal lattice corridors to reach corruption nodes
- Each node is a puzzle: match the original resonance pattern while dissonance tries to overwrite it
- Successful purges cause the dome to "breathe" — visible expansion and contraction

**Corruption Purge**
- Three-stage purge per corrupted zone:
  1. **Identify** — Use Dissonance Lens to map all corruption veins in the structure
  2. **Isolate** — Activate bell tower resonance to contain spread (rhythm mini-game)
  3. **Purify** — Enter micro-giant mode, destroy corruption crystals at their roots
- Failed purge: corruption spreads to an adjacent zone (real consequences, adds urgency)

**Bell Tower Purification**
- Bell towers serve as "immune system" for the grid
- Ringing a purified bell tower creates a resonance shield (visible golden dome) that blocks corruption spread
- Towers must be rung in sequence matching the ley-line geometry — wrong order weakens the shield
- Master purification: ringing 3 connected towers simultaneously creates a permanent ward

### Companion Layer

**Cassian (Introduced Day 3)**
- Appears as a charming scholar studying the corruption
- **Ability:** Analyzes corruption patterns, provides weak-point data
- **Hidden agenda:** Actually planting dissonance crystals (revealed Moon 7)
- **Banter:** Overly helpful, subtly steers player away from certain discoveries
- Experienced players may notice inconsistencies in his advice

**Lirael (Escalation)**
- Dissonance causes her projection to fracture — visual distortion when near corruption
- **New ability:** Can "remember" a structure's pre-corruption state, providing purge targets
- Emotional beats: corruption reminds her of the original Flood — dialogue becomes more urgent

**Milo (Development)**
- Growls near corruption sources (early warning system)
- **New ability:** Can dig through corrupted mud faster than player
- Becomes protective of Lirael — positions between her and corruption sources

### Combat Framework (Moon 2 Exclusive — 2–3 Memorable Boss & Major Elite Encounters)

**Standard Enemies:** Fractal Wraiths (phase through solid matter, drain Aether) + Mirror Wraiths (copy last 3 attacks). Aggressive, target restoration sites. Night +40% spawns. Bell shields safe.

**NEW: 2–3 Production-Quality Moon 2 Boss Encounters (Climactic Cavern Moments)**

These feel like true crescendos in the crystalline caverns, built on BossEncounterSystem + frequency matching + VFX vein/crystal systems (R7 living cathedral polish). Strong telegraphing (vein throb = timer, crystal color strobes = target Hz, dome labored breathing, companion VO). Phases with vuln windows. Permanent world payoffs (purified veins persist visually/mechanically across sessions).

1. **Cathedral Vein Warden** (The "Cathedral Guardian" — signature mid/late Moon 2 climax at moon2_cathedral_dome)
   - **Identity fit:** The corrupted living heart of the central cathedral fights back. Veins spread like fuse across dome walls (dark fractal VFX). Player purges by frequency-tuning crystal facets on the architecture.
   - **Phases (4):** Vein Awakening (exterior spread + 2 nodes, generous first vuln), Reflected Wrath (Mirror Wraiths + crystal shards), Fractal Descent (signature Micro-Giant core entry — player shrinks into lattice while companions defend exterior; Lirael projects nodes, Milo digs, Cassian hints unreliable), Warden's Lament (desperation).
   - **Mechanics:** Frequency puzzle on 4 vein nodes (Hz color telegraph on crystals). Good solves reverse-burn veins golden. Micro-Giant + companion synergy core phase. Giant meter charges from purges.
   - **Telegraphing:** Dome breathing becomes erratic/pained, vein crawl speed = next attack, strobing facets at exact target freq (174 red → 852 violet), VO: "The stone weeps — retune the facets!"
   - **Permanent World Payoff:** Cathedral veins permanently golden/purified. Dome breathing majestic. +22 RS to all Moon2 buildings. Unlocks hidden Architect's Resonance Chamber (bonus harvest). Micro-Giant II duration +25s. Zone corruption immunity in cathedral sector. "The Stone Remembers" achievement.

2. **Fractal Vein Mirror** (Major elite fight in deep crystal caverns — crystal_hall / ley tunnels)
   - **Identity fit:** Recursive crystal mass that weaponizes the environment's own crystals and veins. Moon 2's fractal identity made boss.
   - **Phases (3):** Echo Lattice (mirror copies + freq shift), Shattered Choir (environment crystals awaken as hazards), Resonant Collapse (final core vein).
   - **Mechanics:** Shifting target freq that echoes player's recent solves. Player can "recruit" purified crystals for temporary resonance buffs. Companion: Milo shatters small mirrors.
   - **Telegraphing:** Every crystal in arena pulses in perfect sync with boss current Hz. Vein throb urgency.
   - **Permanent World Payoff:** "Resonant Crystal Grove" — purified crystals remain permanently lit golden. Bonus Aether harvest in sector, -30% Fractal Wraith spawns, passive harmony field.

3. **Dissonance Root Core** (Major/climax encounter at ley_chamber roots + cathedral foundation — ties directly to Moon-End Fractal Corruption Purge)
   - **Identity fit:** Massive corruption root entity erupting from Moon 2's ley veins. First real "loss" threat if grid spread not contained.
   - **Phases (4):** Root Bloom (vein eruptions), Bell Sever (must ring bell towers via freq to weaken), Giant Heart (hybrid Giant Mode surface root tear + Micro-Giant inner core), Final Purge (architect chord sequence).
   - **Mechanics:** Frequency on root hearts + bell tower synergy (area purge). Giant Mode synergy for tearing exposed roots (charges meter from prior fights). Micro-Giant for final heart.
   - **Telegraphing:** Ground veins glow/crawl, distant bells hum warning tones at target freq, "the foundation hungers".
   - **Permanent World Payoff:** Full ley_chamber purification. New fractal passage deeper into caverns. +5% global resonance grid. Bell towers now project permanent area corruption shields. Unlocks Moon 3.

**Engagement Style:** All encounters reward frequency mastery over brute force. Building restoration mid-fight (bell towers, crystal facets) strengthens arena. Micro-Giant / Giant Mode power fantasy moments. Companions provide active synergy (not just banter). Failures spread corruption (real stakes).

**Integration:** Fully wired to BossEncounterSystem (phases, freq Submit, VFX, persistence, Golden Cascade "world sings back"). Moon2BossEncounters.cs provides the definitions + dedicated vein/crystal AI + payoffs. Spawn via "cathedral_vein_warden", "fractal_vein_mirror", "dissonance_root_core".

### Moon-End Spectacle (Day 28)

**The Fractal Corruption Purge** (now powered by Dissonance Root Core + Cathedral Vein Warden elements)
- Massive corruption bloom threatens the Moon 1 cathedral (and Moon 2 caverns bleed through)
- Player must coordinate bell towers, companions, and micro-giant purges simultaneously — can include Root Core or Warden as the living core
- Climax: entering the cathedral's deepest fractal layer reveals a pre-Flood memory — the original architect's final moments as the dissonance struck
- Purge success: cathedral's organ spontaneously plays a chord that resonates across the entire restored grid (enhanced by prior Warden/Mirror victories)
- Unlocks: Moon 3 zone, Micro-Giant Mode II (longer duration), corruption resistance upgrades, permanent Moon 2 vein sanctuaries

**Why these fit Moon 2's identity:** Corruption as internal living threat inside the beautiful crystal cathedral/caverns. Frequency is the language of the world. Micro-Giant + environment (veins you literally retune like fuses). First emotional loss if you let corruption spread. Permanent visual/mechanical catharsis — the caverns remember your song. Cathedral guardian delivers the "this building is alive and fighting" fantasy promised by 12_VIVID_VISUALS / GDD living crystal cathedral.
