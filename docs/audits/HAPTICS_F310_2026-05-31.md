## Trace test — `GameEvents.Fire*` → `SetMotorSpeeds`?

- `FireBuildingRestored(buildingId)` → `OnBuildingRestored` event → subscribers: `AudioFeedbackController` (SFX only), `Moon1AudioAtmosphere`, `Moon1CombatDirector`, `SaveManager`, `BuildingRestorationCeremony`, `LeyLineMap/Minimap`, etc. **None call `HapticFeedbackManager`.** Chain broken.
- `RaiseEnemyKilled(args)` → `OnEnemyKilled` → subscribers: `AudioFeedbackController` (SFX), `VFXWiringController` (VFX), `PlayerProgression` (XP). **None call haptics.** Chain broken.
- Direct calls from mini-games & `ResonanceScannerSystem` to `HapticFeedbackManager.Instance?.Play*()` DO complete the chain to `SetMotorSpeeds` — but they bypass the pub/sub layer entirely.

## Verdict

The **manager is solid** — bootstraps itself, calls `SetMotorSpeeds` correctly, includes the spec's 5s emergence curve, has F310-specific entry points (`TriggerF310Rumble`), supports per-Moon `HapticContext` profiles for Moons 3+. Pattern data lives as numeric literals in code, not data assets — fine for now, will hurt when designers want to tune.

The **wiring is half-done**. Mini-games (Tuning, Pipe Organ, Rail, Choir, Aether Conduit, Rock Cutting) and the Resonance Scanner are wired well — those events fire correctly. The pub/sub bridge from `GameEvents.OnBuildingRestored` / `OnEnemyKilled` to haptics is **not connected** — every subscriber on those events handles audio/VFX/quest progression but never touches haptics. As a result, the spec's headline events — **Building Emergence triggered by restoration, Golem Death, Golem Spawn, Footstep** — never reach a motor on the F310 in the live code paths. Most of the missing wiring is in `.disabled` files (legacy `CombatBridge`, `BossEncounterSystem`, `GameLoopController`) — restoring those would close the gap but is out of scope for a "make haptics work on Moon 1" patch.

For Moon 1 specifically: of the 9 spec events, **4 are fully wired (Discovery, Tuning On/Off, Perfect Tune), 1 is partially wired (Building Emergence — works for mini-games, not for the canonical building-restoration ceremony), 1 is misleadingly wired (Combat Hit fires only on player damage), and 3 are unwired (Footstep, Golem Spawn, Golem Death).**

Top-3 patches above get the missing events vibrating in roughly 10 lines of code.
