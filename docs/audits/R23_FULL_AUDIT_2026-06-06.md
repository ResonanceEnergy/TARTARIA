## TOP 3 SHIPSTOPPERS for Moon 1 single-playthrough completion

1. **Cathedral hero building is FRAGMENTED** — only `Cathedral_Facade` + `Cathedral_Interior` in scene, no unified `Echohaven_Cathedral` root matching the StarDome/CrystalSpire convention. The restoration loop's 3rd hero target cannot trigger. The 17th-hour eruption beat depends on Cathedral being a single addressable object. **Fix:** spawn `Echohaven_Cathedral` parent GameObject containing both fragments.

2. **F5/F9 save/load NOT wired at scene level** — SaveManager.cs exists in scripts but no scene-level bootstrap or input listener. After a crash mid-restoration the playthrough cannot resume. **Fix:** ensure `SaveManager` instance is in scene as a DontDestroyOnLoad bootstrap with a `Keyboard.current[Key.F5]/[Key.F9].wasPressedThisFrame` listener.

3. **Audio stack ABSENT from scene** — no `AdaptiveMusicController`, no ambient hum AudioSource, no 17th-hour music GameObject. Per docs/15 §1 the demo requires "hum grows" at 0:00-1:00 and "music swells" at RS 75. **Fix:** add `AdaptiveMusicController` MonoBehaviour to `Moon1_Systems` parent with the 4 ambient_layer*.wav stems wired.
